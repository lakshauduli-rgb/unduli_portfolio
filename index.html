Profile

I'm a third-year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate specialising in the Aeronautical Stream at the University of Moratuwa, with a strong foundation in aerodynamic design, computational analysis, CAD, and hands-on engineering development. I work across the full design cycle — from selecting and analysing airfoils with XFOIL, XFLR5, MATLAB, and ANSYS, to modelling in SolidWorks, manufacturing composite and precision components, and validating the final outcome through testing. My experience also includes reverse engineering, materials identification, and embedded systems using Python, C++, Arduino, and ESP32.

I combine engineering analysis, creative problem-solving, and hands-on development to turn concepts into practical solutions, with experience spanning mechanical design, aerodynamics, manufacturing, and automation. My work is grounded in practical problem solving: balancing performance with material and process constraints, and contributing effectively within multidisciplinary teams. Alongside technical work, I have developed strong leadership and communication skills through roles in IMechE, IEEE, IESL, and other university organisations, including editorial leadership, event coordination, public speaking, and representing Sri Lankan engineering talent as a SOFE 2025 finalist.
